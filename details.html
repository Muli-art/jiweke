24-Hour Limited Warranty / Freshness Guarantee

Jiweke Tavern is committed to delivering fresh, high-quality meals prepared under strict hygiene standards.

We offer a 24-hour limited guarantee on all food and beverage orders. If you receive food that is:

  • spoiled,
  • incorrectly prepared,
  • unsafe to consume,
  • or significantly different from what you ordered,

you must report the issue within 24 hours of delivery or pickup.

✅ We will inspect your complaint and, where valid, offer a replacement meal or refund.

⚠️ Exclusions:
  • The food was stored incorrectly after delivery,
  • It was consumed or partially eaten,
  • The complaint is made after 24 hours,
  • The food was altered, reheated, or mixed after delivery.
